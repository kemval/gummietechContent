{{ t("It reads the paper, not the press release", translated and "Lee el paper, no el comunicado") }}

{{ t("Most feeds are news about papers, and news coverage inverts mechanisms, overstates what a result overturns, and quotes whoever gave the interview. So before writing anything, the pipeline digs the DOI out of the page, asks Crossref who actually wrote the work, and drafts from the abstract — with the coverage demoted to context and the first author, not the press office, on the credit line.", translated and "La mayoría de las fuentes son noticias sobre papers, y la cobertura invierte mecanismos, exagera lo que un resultado derriba y cita a quien dio la entrevista. {{ t("Then it tries to prove itself wrong", translated and "Después intenta demostrarse equivocado") }}

{{ t("A draft is re-fetched and checked claim by claim against its source: every number and name has to appear there, the limitation on slide four has to be one the paper states about itself, and the researchers' hedges have to survive onto the slide. Una segunda revisión mide la imagen renderizada: texto que cruza el marco, contraste por debajo de 4,5 a 1, una etiqueta de preprint desplazada fuera de vista.
